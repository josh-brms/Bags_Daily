Refund Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Your rights

Under the Philippine Consumer Act (RA 7394), you may return defective, damaged, or wrongly described goods. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

2. Return window

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order. Please inspect your piece as soon as it arrives.

3. Condition of returned items

  • Items should be unworn and unwashed, with tags still attached — unless the item is defective.
  • Defective or wrong items can be returned in the condition they arrived.

4. How to request a return

Email hello@cy.studio with your order details, a short description of the issue, and photos if the item is damaged or defective. We will reply with the next steps, including where to send the item.

5. Remedies

Depending on the situation, we offer a replacement, an exchange, or a refund of the item's price. If a refund is due, it is returned through the payment channel used for the original purchase.

6. Change of mind

For non-defective change-of-mind returns, we offer an exchange or store credit where stock allows. Original delivery fees are not refundable in this case.

7. Contact

Questions about a return? Email hello@cy.studio and we will help.
